Tilak Varma backed up a superb 116* in the first innings with a combative 51 not out to steer
South Zone into the Duleep Trophy final. South will meet the Ishan Kishan's East Zone in the title clash
from September 6 at the MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai.

Tilak, who batted at No. 4 with South having lost two quick wickets just prior to lunch on Wednesday,
put together an unbroken 80-run stand with R Smaran, who finished 31 not out, in the chase of 181. Tilak,
who survived a close lbw shout first ball off left-arm spinner Abid Mushtaq, played largely within himself
until the runs were whittled down under 50, when he expanded his range
